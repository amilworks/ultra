The opsz lever

TWK Lausanne is a static font — one set of proportions at every size. Inter is variable, and its optical-size axis narrows the round letters by about 8.5% as type gets bigger. Ultra sets font-optical-sizing: auto globally, so every heading gets the narrowed display cut. Pinning opsz low keeps Inter's generous text proportions at display size — which is exactly where Lausanne lives.

Lowercase lands within ~2%. What genuinely differs is the uppercase: Lausanne's round caps are wider (O +6.8%) while its straight caps are narrower (H −3.9%) and 1.9% shorter. That is more contrast between round and square capitals than Inter has — an outline property no axis or feature swap can reach. It needs drawing.
